# Password Security Standard

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Password requirements

Passwords for company accounts must have at least 14 characters. Long passphrases are preferred over short complex strings. Reusing a work password on any other service is prohibited. The last 12 passwords cannot be used again.

## Password manager

All staff use the company-provided password manager to generate and store credentials. Storing passwords in spreadsheets, notes, browser profiles on shared machines, or chat messages is not allowed.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN, the HR portal, and every system holding customer data. Authenticator apps or hardware keys are required; SMS codes are permitted only as a temporary fallback for up to 7 days.

## Rotation and resets

Passwords are not changed on a fixed schedule, because forced rotation encourages weak patterns. A password must be changed immediately if compromise is suspected. The IT service desk verifies identity through a video call before resetting credentials and will never ask for a current password.

## Shared and service accounts

Shared personal logins are prohibited. Service accounts are owned by a named team, store secrets in the corporate vault, and have their keys rotated every 90 days.

## Lockout

An account locks for 15 minutes after 10 failed sign-in attempts. Repeated lockouts are investigated as a potential security incident.
