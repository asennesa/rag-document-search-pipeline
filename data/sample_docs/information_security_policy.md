# Information Security Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Data classification

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Customer records and personnel files are Confidential. Payment data, health information, and security keys are Restricted. If in doubt, treat information as Confidential until the data owner decides.

## Handling rules

Confidential data may be shared externally only through the approved file transfer service with an expiry date. Restricted data must be encrypted in storage and in transit and may not be placed on removable media. USB storage is disabled on company laptops by default.

## Access control

Access follows least privilege. Managers review the access of their teams every 6 months. Access is removed within 1 working day after someone leaves the company or changes roles.

## Devices and networks

Laptops use full-disk encryption, and screens lock automatically after 5 minutes. On public Wi-Fi, employees must use the corporate VPN. Laptops must be carried as hand luggage when travelling, never checked in.

## Generative AI tools

Internal, Confidential, or Restricted information must not be entered into public AI assistants. Only tools approved by the security team may be used with company data.

## Training and compliance

All employees complete security awareness training every year. Suspected phishing is forwarded to the security mailbox. Violations may lead to disciplinary action under the Workplace Conduct Policy.
