# Incident Reporting Procedure

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## What to report

Report any event that harms or could harm people, data, or systems. This includes suspected phishing that was clicked, lost devices, data sent to the wrong person, malware alerts, workplace injuries, and near misses. Reporting something that turns out to be harmless is never penalised.

## How quickly

Security incidents must be reported immediately, and in all cases within 1 hour of discovery. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours at the latest, and sooner where possible. Use the 24-hour security hotline or the incident form on the intranet.

## What to do first

Do not delete messages, turn off an affected machine, or try to investigate alone. Disconnect a compromised laptop from the network and leave it powered on. Note what happened and when.

## Severity and response

The security team classifies incidents from Severity 1 (critical) to Severity 4 (minor). Severity 1 incidents have a response lead assigned within 15 minutes. If personal data is affected, the data protection officer assesses whether regulators must be notified within 72 hours.

## Workplace injuries

Injuries are reported to the site safety officer on the day they occur and recorded in the accident book. Call emergency services first if anyone needs urgent care.

## After an incident

A blameless review takes place within 10 working days of closing a Severity 1 or 2 incident, and agreed actions are tracked to completion.
