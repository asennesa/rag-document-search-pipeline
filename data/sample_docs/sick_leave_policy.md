# Sick Leave Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Reporting an absence

Employees who cannot work because of illness must tell their line manager before 09:30 on the first day of absence. A message in the team channel is not sufficient; call or message the manager directly. Share the expected return date as soon as it is known.

## Medical certificates

Absences of up to 3 consecutive calendar days are self-certified. A certificate from a doctor is required from the fourth calendar day. Certificates are uploaded to the HR portal and are visible only to HR. Managers are told how long the absence will last, not its medical reason.

## Sick pay

Employees receive full pay for the first 20 working days of sickness in any rolling 12-month period, and 50 percent of pay for the following 40 working days. After that, statutory benefits apply. Sick pay begins after the probation period; during probation only statutory sick pay applies.

## Caring for dependants

Up to 5 paid days per calendar year may be used to care for a sick child or dependant. These days are not deducted from annual leave.

## Returning to work

After an absence longer than 10 working days the employee and manager hold a return-to-work conversation. A phased return of up to 4 weeks at reduced hours on full pay may be agreed. Working from home during recovery is arranged under the Remote Work Policy, and being ill is never by itself a reason to expect someone to work remotely.
