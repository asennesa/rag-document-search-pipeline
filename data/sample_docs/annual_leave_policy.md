# Annual Leave Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Entitlement

Employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rated entitlement based on their contracted hours. Employees who join or leave during the year accrue leave at 2.08 days for each completed month of service.

## Requesting leave

Leave requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 10 working days before the first day of absence. Requests of more than 10 consecutive working days need approval from both the line manager and the department head. Managers respond within 5 working days. Two people in the same small team may be refused overlapping leave if minimum staffing cannot be maintained.

## Carry-over

Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next calendar year and must be used by 31 March. Days above that limit are forfeited unless illness or parental leave prevented the employee from taking them. Unused leave cannot normally be exchanged for pay, except for days outstanding when employment ends.

## Illness during leave

An employee who becomes ill during annual leave may reclassify those days as sick leave by providing a medical certificate within 5 working days of returning. See the Sick Leave Policy for the certificate rules.

## Company shutdown

The company closes between 24 December and 1 January. Three days of each employee's annual entitlement are reserved for this shutdown. Staff in on-call roles who work during the shutdown keep those days.
