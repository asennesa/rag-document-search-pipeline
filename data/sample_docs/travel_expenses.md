# Expense Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## General rules

The company reimburses reasonable costs paid personally for work. Claims are submitted in the finance system within 30 calendar days of the expense, with an itemised receipt for anything above 10 EUR. Late claims need approval from the department head. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly salary run.

## Meals during travel

While away overnight on business, meals are reimbursed up to 60 EUR per day, based on actual receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at client meals approved in advance.

## Client entertainment

Client meals are limited to 80 EUR per person. The claim must list the attendees and the business purpose.

## Mileage and transport

Use of a private car on business is reimbursed at 0.35 EUR per kilometre. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable. Taxis are reimbursed when public transport is unavailable, unsafe, or impractical with luggage.

## Home office and small purchases

The one-time home office allowance of 300 EUR is claimed here with receipts. Purchases above 500 EUR must go through procurement rather than personal payment.

## Corporate cards and misuse

Corporate cards are for business costs only. Personal spending on a corporate card must be repaid within 10 working days. Knowingly submitting a false claim is treated as gross misconduct.
