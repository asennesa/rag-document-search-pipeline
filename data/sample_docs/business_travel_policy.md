# Business Travel Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Approval

Every business trip needs line manager approval before booking. International trips also need department head approval at least 14 calendar days before departure. Trips to countries under a company travel restriction need written sign-off from the security team.

## Booking

Book through the company travel portal so that the traveller can be located in an emergency. Economy class is the standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed on longer flights. Train travel is preferred over flying for journeys shorter than 4 hours.

## Hotels

Hotels are limited to 150 EUR per night, or 220 EUR in cities on the high-cost list. Stays above the limit need prior approval from the department head.

## Travel time and wellbeing

Travel outside normal hours on a weekend earns one day of time off in lieu, taken within 3 months. Employees should not drive more than 4 hours in a single day after a working day, and a rental car is used in place of a private car for trips above 300 kilometres.

## Safety and insurance

The company's insurance covers medical emergencies and lost luggage on approved trips. Keep the emergency assistance number available. Report stolen devices or documents under the Incident Reporting Procedure. Costs incurred on the trip are claimed under the Expense Policy.
