# Employee Equipment Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Standard equipment

Every employee receives a laptop, a headset, and a mobile phone if their role requires one. Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Equipment is ordered by IT during onboarding and is normally ready on the first working day.

## Repairs and replacement

Report a faulty device to the IT service desk. If a laptop battery no longer holds a charge, or a device will not turn on, IT provides a loan device within 2 working days while it is repaired. Employees must not open a device or use unofficial repair shops, since this voids the warranty.

## Loss, theft, and damage

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT and to security within 24 hours under the Incident Reporting Procedure so that the device can be remotely wiped. Theft requires a police report. Employees do not pay for accidental damage, but repeated negligence may be raised with their manager.

## Personal use

Reasonable personal use of a company laptop is permitted outside working hours. Installing software not approved by IT is prohibited. Family members may not use company devices.

## Returning equipment

All equipment is returned within 5 working days of the last day of employment. Employees who are remote receive a prepaid courier label. Unreturned items may be deducted from the final salary where the contract allows it.
