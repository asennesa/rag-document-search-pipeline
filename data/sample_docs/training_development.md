# Training and Development Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Annual budget

Every employee has a personal learning budget of 1,200 EUR per calendar year, plus 5 paid learning days. Unused budget does not carry over. Part-time employees receive the full monetary budget and pro-rated learning days.

## Approval

Courses and conferences costing less than 500 EUR need manager approval only. Anything above 500 EUR also needs HR approval and a short statement of how it relates to the employee's role. Travel for a course is booked under the Business Travel Policy and does not count against the learning budget.

## Certifications and degrees

The company pays exam fees for role-related certifications, including one retake. Part-time degree programmes may receive up to 50 percent funding, capped at 4,000 EUR per year, after 2 years of service.

## Repayment

If an employee resigns within 12 months of completing training that cost more than 2,000 EUR, they repay a pro-rated share of the cost. The agreement is signed before the course begins.

## Mentoring

Any employee may ask HR for a mentor from another department. Mentoring pairs meet at least monthly for 6 months. Time spent on mentoring counts as working time and does not use learning days.

## Mandatory training

Mandatory compliance courses, including annual security awareness training, are paid separately and never use the personal learning budget or learning days.
