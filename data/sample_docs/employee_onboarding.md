# Employee Onboarding Guide

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Before the first day

HR sends the employment contract and a welcome pack at least 10 working days before the start date. The manager assigns a buddy, a colleague from another team who answers everyday questions, and IT prepares equipment and accounts.

## First day

New employees arrive at reception at 09:00 with a government-issued identity document. They collect their laptop, set up multi-factor authentication, and meet their team. Payroll details are entered into the HR portal on the same day.

## First week

Within the first 5 working days, new employees complete the mandatory courses on security awareness, data protection, and workplace conduct. Access to customer systems is granted only after these courses are finished.

## Probation

Probation lasts 6 months. The manager holds review conversations at 1, 3, and 5 months. Either party may end the employment with 2 weeks' notice during probation. Probation may be extended once by up to 3 months when more time is needed. During probation employees work from the office and do not receive company sick pay beyond the statutory amount.

## 30-60-90 day goals

Each new employee agrees written goals for the first 30, 60, and 90 days. At 90 days, the employee gives feedback on the onboarding experience through an anonymous survey.
