# Remote Work Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to their role. Warehouse, reception, and on-site laboratory roles are not eligible because the work cannot be done away from the site. During probation, employees work from the office.

## Core hours and availability

Remote employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 local office time. Tuesday is the team anchor day and everyone eligible attends the office unless on leave or travelling.

## Working from another country

Working from abroad is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and needs written approval from HR at least 15 working days in advance, because of tax and insurance implications. Countries under company travel restrictions are excluded. This is not the same as a business trip, which follows the Business Travel Policy.

## Home office requirements

Employees need a private workspace and a reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps. A one-time home office allowance of 300 EUR covers a chair, desk, or monitor arm; receipts are claimed under the Expense Policy. Company laptops must connect through the corporate VPN whenever they access internal systems, and confidential papers must not be left where household members can read them.

## Ending an arrangement

Either the employee or the manager may end a remote arrangement with 30 calendar days' notice. Performance concerns are discussed before any change is made.
