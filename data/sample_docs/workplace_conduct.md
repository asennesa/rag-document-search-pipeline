# Workplace Conduct Policy

> Fictional sample document for a portfolio demonstration. Lumen Harbor Ltd does not exist.

## Respect at work

Everyone is expected to treat colleagues, customers, and suppliers with respect. Harassment, bullying, and discrimination are not tolerated, whether in person, in chat channels, or at work social events.

## Raising a concern

Concerns may be raised with a line manager, HR, or through the confidential ethics hotline, which accepts anonymous reports. Investigations begin within 5 working days. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare outside work, financial interests in suppliers, or close relationships with someone in your reporting line. A second paid job must be declared to HR and must not compete with the company.

## Gifts and hospitality

Gifts worth up to 50 EUR may be accepted. Anything above this must be recorded in the gift register and approved by the department head. Cash gifts are never acceptable, regardless of value.

## Alcohol and social media

Being impaired by alcohol or drugs at work is prohibited. At company events alcohol is served in moderation. When posting on social media, do not share confidential information or speak for the company unless authorised.

## Disciplinary process

Breaches may result in a verbal warning, a written warning, a final written warning, or dismissal. Gross misconduct, such as theft, fraud, or violence, may lead to immediate dismissal. Employees may bring a colleague or representative to any disciplinary meeting.
